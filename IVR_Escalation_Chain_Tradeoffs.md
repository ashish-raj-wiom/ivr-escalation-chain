## Rejected framings worth remembering

Two things were considered and dropped for reasons that are not decisions about the product, but are worth not repeating:

- **"The IVR is 24.8pp worse than a direct call for customer-initiated calls."** Withdrawn. The non-IVR figure on the ops dashboard is **call-level**, and the IVR figures used here are **ticket-level** — the comparison mixed grains. Non-IVR data is not to be used as a ticket-level benchmark anywhere.
- **Deriving the target from independence — 1 − 0.5³ = 87.5%.** Rejected as a target. Three rungs each answering at ~50% only compounds like that if the three failures are unrelated; a common cause would suppress all three at once. MQ-3 measures per-rung answer rate so this is settled with evidence within a month of launch instead of assumed at sign-off.
